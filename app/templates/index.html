Profile Photo Analyzer

Upload a photo. The analyzer checks that it has real content, finds every face, and verifies which faces are real human faces (animals, birds, cartoons, paintings and diagrams are counted separately). For each real human face it estimates the age in 3-year brackets and the gender. Anything the models cannot support is reported as Unsure or Not Detected.
